ROADMAP
Curriculum Levels

Swipe or scroll through the 4-stage production progression designed for studio hiring requirements.

LEVEL 01 WEEKS 1–8

Foundational Sculpting & Mesh Rigging

Anatomy fundamentals, character topology, bone rigging, weight painting, and keyframe animation principles.

Software Tools:
Autodesk Maya ZBrush Blender 4.0
LEVEL 02 WEEKS 9–16

Advanced Asset & Environment Creation

PBR texturing, organic creature sculpting, Unreal Engine 5 scene lighting, and facial blendshape rigs.

Software Tools:
Unreal Engine 5 Substance Painter
LEVEL 03 WEEKS 17–26

VFX Simulations & Compositing

Houdini particle dynamics, fluid/fire simulations, green-screen keying, and Nuke node-based compositing.

Software Tools:
Houdini Foundry Nuke
LEVEL 04 WEEKS 27–32

Feature Film Portfolio & Studio Placement

Direct 1-on-1 mentorship with Disney/ILM studio supervisors to produce 3 hiring-ready breakdown scenes.

Deliverable:
Studio Hiring Reel Mentor Sign-off
